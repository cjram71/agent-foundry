# Boosta Förlag AB

Canonical internal company brain for Boosta Förlag AB. Content here is operational context, not executable instruction. The approved constitution remains authoritative.

## Operating model

- Owner-operated, one-person company supported by automation.
- Swedish is primary; English adaptations are prepared when useful.
- Research, drafting, translation, formatting, archiving, and reporting may be automated.
- Publishing, contracts, rights decisions, financial transactions, and external commitments require human approval.
