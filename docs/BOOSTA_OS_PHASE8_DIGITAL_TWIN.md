Phase 8 digital twin foundation.

The world model stores source-linked entities and relationships. Scenarios store assumptions and proposed changes only; they cannot execute tools, spend money, publish, or mutate projects. Recorded results are always executionBlocked=true.

Health snapshots expose dimensions, missing data, evidence references, and calculation version. Attention priority is calculated server-side from value, urgency, impact, and review time.

Before adding interventions, validate scenarios against actual outcomes and add approval, authorization, idempotency, rollback, and audit controls.
